Pollen Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Thank you for using Pollen. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

Information We Collect

Pollen is designed to help users learn languages while respecting their privacy.

  • Your learning progress
  • Vocabulary progress
  • Quiz statistics
  • Study streaks
  • Application preferences (theme, language, settings)

This information is stored locally on your device unless otherwise stated.

Personal Information

Pollen does not require users to create an account. We do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, address, or phone number.

Analytics

Pollen may use anonymous analytics to improve the application experience. These analytics never include sensitive personal information.

Third-Party Services

The application may use trusted third-party services such as Google Play Services and Text-to-Speech engines provided by your device. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

Advertisements

Pollen does not display third-party advertisements.

Children's Privacy

Pollen is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information and continuously improve the security of the application.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information where applicable.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page.
